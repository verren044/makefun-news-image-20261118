Sum up 18 November's world news in one image — a newspaper front page
carrying the whole day: 52 stories, a world map and a UTC timeline.

## The day in one page

- **4 illustrated scenes**: the Gulf tanker war, the Kerch Bridge struck
  a second night, APEC opening in Shenzhen, COP31 in Antalya.
- **9 promoted anchors**: Peltola wins Alaska (Democrats take the Senate
  56-44) plus 8 large cards — Nvidia 115.8 BN, Wall Street falls again,
  Ebola doubles, Cyclone Xavier, the dirty bomb report, Gaza truce at
  risk, the Myanmar court ruling, US tech outages.
- **39 briefs** across 7 regions, every one a real event of Wednesday
  18 November 2026, verified against that day's news roundups.
- **World map** with 8 marked locations and a **UTC timeline** of the
  day's 6 turning points (00 Kerch, 03 Gulf, 08 APEC, 09 COP31,
  15 Wall Street, 21 Gaza).

## How it runs

Pure Python standard library only — `python3 main.py` renders
`output.png` (1024x1024, deterministic: two runs are byte-identical).
No network, no keys, no randomness.
